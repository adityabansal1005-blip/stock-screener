    How to use this

    Run RESEARCH.cmd from the project folder to inspect a local historical archive. A fresh download contains no private history. Ask your coding assistant to read docs/FUND_PROTOCOL.md for the frozen rules and explicit diagnostic command. Unavailable returns are not zero returns.

    Legacy diagnostic portfolios use normalized units because historical price adjustments are unverified. They do not establish real-share execution or total-return performance. Historical tests cannot certify future profit.
